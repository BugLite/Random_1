Land Pollution?

Land Pollution is the pollution of the land, soil, and other natural resources. It can be caused by industrial waste, agricultural chemicals, and other human activities.

Water Pollution?

Water Pollution is the pollution of the water, which can be caused by industrial waste, agricultural chemicals, and other human activities.

Air Pollution?

Air Pollution is the pollution of the air, which can be caused by industrial waste, agricultural chemicals, and other human activities.

What Can Humanity Do to Combat Pollution?

Everyone can help — young or old! Here’s how:

  • ♻️ Recycle paper, plastic, and glass.
  • 🌿 Plant trees and care for plants.
  • 🚴 Ride bikes or walk more.
  • 🌊 Don't throw trash into water or on land.
  • 🔌 Save energy — turn off lights and devices when not needed.
  • 🗣️ Talk about it — tell friends and family how to help.

Small steps by many people can make a BIG difference!
